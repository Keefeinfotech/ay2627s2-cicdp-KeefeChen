Auxilia · Round 5 · Product Design Research & Development

Three directions, one secretary.

Auxilia — “Help Anywhere, Learn Together” — is an AI-assisted productivity assistant for students. Round 5 compared six Mobbin references, extracted a design DNA, and expressed it as three genuinely different interfaces. All three are recognisably Auxilia: Trust Blue core, Roboto, green as the completion signal, no purple, no emoji.

DIRECTION A — SECRETARY'S DESK

Warm editorial productivity

The dependable human secretary: warm ivory paper, deep navy ink, Trust Blue actions, a 264px labelled sidebar.

Sidebar navMedium densityWarm paper ivoryCards with soft shadow
  • Editorial hierarchy, generous 16–24px rhythm
  • Best for: students who want calm structure and a sense of a person
  • Closest to shipped app (sidebar + stat cards) but warmer and calmer

DIRECTION B — QUIET STUDIO

Radical restraint

One centered column, hairline rules, underline tab navigation, near-zero chrome. The interface disappears; the work remains.

Top underline tabsLowest densityCool off-whiteHairline dividers, no cards
  • Light-weight typography, maximal whitespace
  • Best for: focus-first students, reading-heavy weeks
  • Takes cues from Notion/Linear minimalism over dashboards

DIRECTION C — COMMAND CENTER

High-density dark deck

Deep navy console: 64px icon rail, 12-column panel grid, mono tabular numerals, status line, Ctrl+K. Everything visible at once.

Icon rail navHighest densityDeep navyMono data, 12-col grid
  • Power-user framing: sync status, activity log, keyboard-first
  • Best for: CS students who live in terminals and dashboards
  • Strongest “AI operations” story; heavier cognitive load

Recommendation: ship A, borrow B's density discipline and C's keyboard path. A matches the shipped Flutter app's structure (sidebar + stat grid) with the warmth the brand needs; B's hairline-and-whitespace restraint fixes the “dull” flatness flagged in Round 5 critique; C's Ctrl+K and sync status line are worth porting into A.
